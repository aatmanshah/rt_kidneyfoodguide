RenalTracker AI — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 9, 2026
Last Updated: November 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to RenalTracker AI ("RenalTracker," "we," "our," or "us"), operated by Catalyst Forge LLC.

RenalTracker is an AI-powered nutrition platform designed to help individuals manage dietary considerations associated with Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) and co-occurring conditions such as diabetes and high blood pressure.

Our platform uses information voluntarily provided by users, including health profiles, dietary preferences, and nutritional requirements, to generate personalized meal plans, food recommendations, and organized grocery lists.

We understand that health information is highly personal and sensitive. Protecting user privacy is a priority. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed, how it is used, and the safeguards we apply.

2. Information We Process

To provide personalized nutrition recommendations, RenalTracker may process information voluntarily submitted by users, including:

Health and Dietary Information

  • Chronic Kidney Disease stage and kidney health information.
  • Diabetes status and related dietary considerations.
  • High blood pressure and other relevant health conditions.
  • Nutritional targets and restrictions, including sodium, potassium, phosphorus, protein, and carbohydrates.
  • Food allergies, intolerances, and dietary restrictions.
  • Food preferences, disliked ingredients, and preferred meals.
  • Other information voluntarily submitted through health and nutrition questionnaires.

AI Interactions

When using RenalTracker's AI-powered features, users may submit questions, health information, and dietary preferences. This information is processed to generate personalized meal plans, food recommendations, and grocery lists.

Technical Information

We may temporarily process limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the platform, such as browser information, device information, and error diagnostics.

3. Saved Meal Plans and Retention

When you generate a meal plan, RenalTracker saves it.

Each saved plan includes:

  • The health and dietary information you submitted for that plan, such as CKD stage, dialysis type, diabetes and high blood pressure status, dietary restrictions, ingredients to avoid, potassium and sodium settings, and any notes you typed.
  • The generated meal plan and grocery list.
  • The date and time the plan was created, and a randomly generated plan ID.

RenalTracker does not currently require an account, and saved plans are not linked to your name or email address. We do not save the text of the AI prompt separately, but the information used to build it is part of the saved plan described above. We do not maintain user medical histories or sell user health information.

Each saved plan is identified by a long, randomly generated plan ID. Anyone who has a plan's ID can retrieve that plan, including the health and dietary information used to create it, so keep plan IDs private.

How long we keep plans: we keep saved plans until they are deleted. We do not currently delete them automatically after a set period.

How to delete your data: use the "Delete all my data" button, available on every screen of the app. It permanently deletes from our servers every plan created in your browser, and erases your saved answers, plan IDs, and grocery check marks from your browser. For plans created on another device or browser, or after you cleared your browser data, email support@renaltracker.com with the plan ID (shown on the plan screen), and we will delete the plan within 30 days.

Some third-party technology providers involved in processing requests, such as AI and hosting providers, may handle or temporarily retain information as necessary to provide their services. Their retention and processing practices are governed by their applicable privacy policies, configurations, and contractual terms.

We take reasonable steps to minimize the information we collect and to avoid unnecessary retention.

4. How We Use Information

Information submitted to RenalTracker is processed for the following purposes:

  • Generating personalized, multi-day meal plans.
  • Creating organized grocery lists.
  • Providing food and ingredient recommendations.
  • Accounting for dietary considerations associated with CKD, diabetes, and high blood pressure.
  • Tailoring recommendations to user-provided nutritional requirements and food preferences.
  • Operating, securing, and troubleshooting the platform.

We do not use submitted health information to create advertising profiles or sell it to third parties.

5. AI Processing and Third-Party Providers

RenalTracker uses artificial intelligence technologies and third-party service providers to generate personalized nutrition recommendations.

Depending on the platform configuration, these providers may include:

  • AI and machine-learning service providers.
  • Cloud hosting and infrastructure providers.
  • Technical monitoring and security providers.
  • Payment processing providers, if applicable.

Information submitted by users may be transmitted to these providers for processing as necessary to fulfill a request.

We aim to use providers with appropriate privacy and security protections. We do not authorize third-party AI providers to use identifiable user health information submitted through RenalTracker to train their general-purpose AI models unless we obtain appropriate consent or otherwise have a lawful basis to do so.

Third-party providers may have their own limited data-retention practices. Users should review the applicable policies of those providers where relevant.

6. HIPAA and Health Information Privacy

RenalTracker recognizes the importance of protecting sensitive health information and is committed to implementing privacy and security practices informed by the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA).

We apply safeguards intended to protect information processed through our platform, including minimizing data collection, limiting unnecessary retention, and using appropriate technical and organizational security measures.

Where HIPAA applies to our activities or relationships, we intend to comply with the applicable HIPAA Privacy, Security, and Breach Notification requirements.

HIPAA applicability and compliance depend on the nature of the services provided, the parties involved, and the applicable legal requirements. Nothing in this policy should be interpreted as establishing that RenalTracker is a HIPAA-covered entity or business associate unless that status has been independently established.

7. Sharing of Information

RenalTracker does not sell users' personal health information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers solely as necessary to operate the platform and provide requested functionality.

We may also disclose information:

  • When required by applicable law or valid legal process.
  • When reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, or property of RenalTracker or its users.
  • With user consent or authorization where appropriate.

We do not share submitted health information with third parties for their independent advertising or marketing purposes without the authorization required by applicable law.

8. Cookies and Technical Information

RenalTracker may use limited cookies or similar technologies to support platform functionality, security, and performance.

These technologies may be used to maintain sessions, remember preferences, and troubleshoot technical issues.

The RenalTracker app also stores your answers, the IDs of the plans you generate, and your grocery-list check marks in your browser's local storage on your own device, so they are there the next time you visit. You can erase all of this at any time with the "Delete all my data" button, or through your browser settings.

We do not intentionally use health information submitted through the platform to build advertising profiles.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information processed through RenalTracker against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

These safeguards may include access controls, secure transmission, encryption where appropriate, and other security measures.

Although we take reasonable steps to protect information, no method of electronic transmission or processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. User Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including:

  • Requesting access to personal information we maintain.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information.
  • Requesting deletion of personal information we maintain.
  • Requesting information about how personal information is processed.
  • Opting out of certain forms of data processing where applicable law provides that right.

Saved plans are not linked to your name or email address, so we cannot look them up by who you are. You can delete every plan created in your browser with the "Delete all my data" button; for any other plan, to access, correct, or delete it we need its plan ID.

AI-generated recommendations may contain errors, omissions, or information that is not appropriate for every individual.

RenalTracker is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. It does not replace the guidance of a physician, nephrologist, registered dietitian, or other qualified healthcare professional.

Users should consult their healthcare professionals before making significant dietary changes or relying on recommendations generated by the platform.
