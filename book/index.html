About RenalTracker

Helping kidney patients enjoy food again.

RenalTracker was founded in 2018 after our founders experienced the personal loss of a loved one to chronic kidney disease. Motivated by that experience, they set out to create a platform that provides practical, evidence-based nutrition education to help people living with CKD make confident food choices. Our mission is simple: to empower kidney patients with the knowledge, recipes, and tools they need to delay, or even defy dialysis entirely, while continuing to enjoy the foods they love.

Frequently asked questions

Who is this book for? +

The Kidney-Friendly Food List is designed for adults living with chronic kidney disease, as well as the caregivers and family members who cook for them.

What CKD stages does it support? +

Food lists and guidance are written with Stage 3 and Stage 4 CKD in mind. Always check with your care team or renal dietitian about what's right for your specific stage and lab results.

Does this work for dialysis patients? +

Yes! Many of our readers are on dialysis. Adjusting your diet to eat CKD-friendly foods can make a major impact on your eGFR, creatinine, and uACR levels.
