The problem

Fall shouldn't mean choosing between safe and special.

There's something special about fall — crisp mornings, cinnamon in the kitchen, and gathering around the table with people you love. Living with kidney disease shouldn't mean giving up the joy that comes from sharing a warm, homemade meal.

Holiday food feels off-limits

Pumpkin pie, stuffing, and mashed potatoes can seem incompatible with a renal diet — so the season's favorites get skipped entirely.

Most fall recipes ignore CKD

Seasonal recipe blogs rarely account for sodium, potassium, or phosphorus — so you're left guessing or substituting on your own.

"Kidney-friendly" sounds bland

Cinnamon, maple, sage, and rosemary do a lot of the work flavor usually gets from salt — this collection leans on them fully.

Harvest Recipes brings the comfort of the season to your table, one kidney-safe recipe at a time.

About RenalTracker

Helping kidney patients enjoy food again.

RenalTracker was founded in 2018 after our founders experienced the personal loss of a loved one to chronic kidney disease. Motivated by that experience, they set out to create a platform that provides practical, evidence-based nutrition education to help people living with CKD make confident food choices. Our mission is simple: to empower kidney patients with the knowledge, recipes, and tools they need to delay, or even defy dialysis entirely, while continuing to enjoy the foods they love.
